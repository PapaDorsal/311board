How we counted

    Why abandoned vehicles

    • It is the highest-volume request type where the closure timestamp plausibly means the work happened: zero same-second closures in 2025, zero cancellations, and every ward files enough of them to measure.
    • Graffiti removal has more volume but closes in under a day citywide, with three wards’ medians pinned at exactly zero by what looks like bulk administrative closure. There is no legible story in a race everyone finishes before lunch.
    • Two request types were set aside on principle: informational calls (662,321 of them are logged at the 311 call center’s own address, in Ward 28) and aircraft noise complaints (364,900 logged at O’Hare, in Ward 41). Those records describe a building, not a neighborhood.
