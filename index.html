How we counted

    What’s on the board, and what isn’t
    • The board tracks request types where the closure timestamp plausibly means work happened - a tow, a filled pothole, a baited alley. Informational calls and aircraft noise complaints are off: the city logs those at its own buildings (the 311 call center in Ward 28, O’Hare in Ward 41), so they describe a facility, not a neighborhood.
    • Some types close almost instantly citywide. We show them anyway and say so - a race everyone finishes before lunch is still a public record.
    • Only 2024, 2025, and the rolling 12 months are offered: the ward map changed in May 2023, and comparing across it would compare different areas under the same ward numbers.
