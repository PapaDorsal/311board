    How we counted

      What’s on the board, and what isn’t
      • The board tracks request types where a closure plausibly means work happened - a tow, a filled pothole, a baited alley. Informational calls and aircraft noise are off: the city logs those at its own buildings (the 311 call center in Ward 28, O’Hare in Ward 41), so they describe a facility, not a neighborhood.
      • Some types close almost instantly citywide. We show them anyway, and say so on the board.
      • Sidewalk repairs are ranked on what the city has not finished: it completes too few to time, and leaves about a third open. Every other type here closes eventually.
      • Bike lane obstructions are off the board - about 630 a year across all 50 wards, far too few to compare wards on. Ward pages carry bike crashes and route mileage as context instead, never as a rank.
      • Only 2024, 2025 and the rolling 12 months are offered: the ward map changed in May 2023, and comparing across it would compare different areas under the same ward numbers.
